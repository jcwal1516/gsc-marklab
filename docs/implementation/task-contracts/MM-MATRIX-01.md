# MM-MATRIX-01 — Bayesian Gaussian matrix factors

Own `BayesianMatrixFactorization` through IC-0168 and `marklab multimodal matrix-factor`. Pinned
mofapy2 0.7.4 trains on observed entries of a bounded patient Gaussian matrix. The missing-command
red preceded implementation; a 16x4 rank-one fixture recovers four masked entries below RMSE 0.35,
improves ELBO, and retains an active aligned factor. Claims remain synthetic and variational; no
non-Gaussian, exact-posterior, real-patient, or biological claim is authorized.
